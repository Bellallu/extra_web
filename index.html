Our Story

A Little Bakery
with a Big Oven

We started Crust & Crumb in a small kitchen on Maple Street. Just a family, a stone-ground flour order, and way too much sourdough starter.

Now we bake every morning before the sun comes up. The bread is ready when you are.

Everything here is made by hand. No machines doing the heavy lifting. Just time, good flour, and a warm oven.

Why It Tastes Different

The Way We Do Things

Natural Fermentation

We let the dough do its thing. 24 to 48 hours, slow and steady. It builds flavour you can't fake.

Quality Flour

Stone-ground from a local mill. You can smell the difference the moment you walk through the door.

No Shortcuts

If it's not right, it stays in the kitchen. We'd rather bake again than hand you something that's just okay.
