 Create a website for a modern artisan bakery called “Crust & Crumb”. The goal is to showcase handmade baked goods, tell a short story about the bakery, and encourage visitors to come in or place pre-orders.
The style should feel warm, cozy, and inviting, like a bakery early in the morning. Use soft cream, white, dark brown, and golden colors. The atmosphere should focus on bread, wood textures, and natural light.
The writing tone should be simple and natural. Use short sentences and sensory words (like warm, soft, crispy). Speak to the visitor like a neighbor, not a customer.
The website should include these sections: a hero section with the headline “Baked Before Sunrise” and buttons for Menu and Order; an about section with a short story of the bakery; a menu section with categories (Bread, Pastries, Cakes) and cards that show image, name, short description, and price; a features section highlighting natural fermentation, quality flour, and no shortcuts; a pre-order section promoting Saturday bread with a call-to-action; a testimonials section with a few short quotes; a contact section with address, opening hours, and a simple form; and a footer with logo, links, and social media.
Include 5–10 images such as bread, croissants, pastries, baker hands, and bakery interior. The images should have warm lighting and focus on texture.
Use simple UI components like cards, tabs for the menu, a carousel for testimonials, and a basic form. Add light hover animations on cards.
Make the design responsive so it works well on mobile, with layouts adjusting from 4 columns to 2 to 1.
The final result should be a clean, warm, and visually appealing website that feels like a real bakery.
